Inbox

An OatMenuBar of commands above an OatSplitLayout: drag the corner of the list to resize it. The list is an OatLoadMoreList that loads the next 20 messages as you scroll. Right-click a message (or press Shift+F10 on it) for its contextMenu(). The icons are OatIcons, and the banner at the bottom is an OatCookieConsent (show it again).
